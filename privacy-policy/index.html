Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you share with us.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Who we are

ZEVON Mobility (“ZEVON”, “we”) provides mobility and transportation services including airport transfers, corporate transport, car rentals, outstation travel, buses and coaches, and fleet partnerships. This policy explains what information we collect through zevonmobility.com and how we use it. Contact: info@zevonmobility.com.

Information we collect

  • Details you submit in booking, contact, corporate and partner forms: name, mobile number, email address, company name, trip details, pickup and drop locations, dates, passenger and vehicle requirements, and any message you write.
  • Basic technical data your browser sends when it loads the site or third-party services described below.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry, check availability, prepare a quotation and arrange the service.
  • To contact you about your request by phone, WhatsApp or email.
  • To keep internal records of enquiries, bookings and partner applications, and to prevent spam or duplicate submissions.

Where your data is stored

Form submissions are sent to ZEVON’s Google Sheets through Google Apps Script and are stored in Google’s services. Access is limited to authorised ZEVON staff. We do not sell your personal information.

Maps and third-party services

When you type or select pickup and destination locations, the text you enter and the coordinates may be sent to third-party map services to find places, draw a route and estimate distance (for example OpenStreetMap-based geocoding and routing services). Map tiles are loaded from OpenFreeMap using OpenMapTiles and OpenStreetMap data. This site also loads fonts from Google Fonts and the MapLibre library from a public CDN, so your IP address is visible to those providers. Route distance and time are estimates only.

Sharing

We share information only as needed to deliver the service, for example with a driver or partner vehicle operator assigned to your trip, or where required by law.

Retention

We keep enquiry and booking records for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain business records and meet legal obligations, and then delete or anonymise them.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information you have submitted by emailing info@zevonmobility.com. You can also use the optional fields sparingly; we do not require more than is needed to handle your request.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including server-side validation of submissions, but no online system is completely secure.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
